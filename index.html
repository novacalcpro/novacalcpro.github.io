Frequently Asked Questions

What is a scientific calculator?

A scientific calculator is designed for mathematical operations beyond basic addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, including trigonometry, logarithms, powers, roots, and factorials.

What is the difference between DEG and RAD mode?

DEG mode uses degrees for trigonometric calculations, while RAD mode uses radians. Select the mode that matches the angle unit in your calculation.

Can I use NovaCalc Pro on a phone?
